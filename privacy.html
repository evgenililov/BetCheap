Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

BetCheap is an informational sports-odds display and comparison website. This page describes data handling currently identifiable from the application. It is not a substitute for a full privacy compliance assessment.

Information processed

When you upload a screenshot for SNAP, the app is designed to process the image in your browser using optional image-recognition software, rather than uploading the screenshot to BetCheap's own server. Search and event requests are sent to BetCheap's serverless endpoints and configured sports-data providers. These requests and normal web access may expose technical information such as your IP address to hosting and service providers.

Odds alerts and local data

The first version of Smart Odds Alert stores the configured alert locally in your browser, checks odds while the page is open and visible, and does not send background alerts after the page closes. Your browser's settings can remove locally stored data.

Optional referral measurement

Visitors using a Champion Z or BetCheap X campaign link may opt into first-party measurement. Before you allow it, no campaign measurement requests or campaign identifiers are stored by this feature. With permission, a random session token is held in sessionStorage for this tab for up to 24 hours. We record the campaign code, session start date and whether a visit, comparison or click to each operator occurred. These records do not include account details, bet information, screenshots or raw IP addresses. Hosting providers still process normal request metadata. These are unverified browser events, not evidence of customers or bets. You can stop future measurement using the on-page Stop measurement button; previously recorded events remain until deletion. Records older than 31 days are scheduled for daily deletion, subject to successful execution. Closing the tab clears its browser session data. Declining measurement does not limit access to comparisons.

Data providers and external links

BetCheap uses Netlify for hosting and may request event and odds data from authorised data providers, including 1xBet. An approved referral link may send you to an independent operator with its own privacy terms. Do not assume BetCheap controls the operator's site.

Affiliate disclosure

BetCheap may earn a commission from eligible, approved referrals. Commissions do not determine the display order of odds. Unapproved or unverified operator redirects remain unavailable.

Retention, rights and contact

We have not confirmed every retention period or third-party logging practice. Requests regarding personal information and privacy can be sent using the contact details on our Contact page once the official support channel is published.

Children

BetCheap is intended for adults aged 18 or older, subject to higher local legal age requirements. It does not accept bets or deposits.
